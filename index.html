Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Portal is a peer-to-peer real estate marketplace where owners, buyers, tenants and brokers find each other and talk directly. This explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and what control you have — in plain language.

"We", "us" and "Portal" mean Portal by UJwal, operated by G. Ujwal as a sole proprietor, based in Warangal, India. "You" means anyone who uses Portal.

1. What we collect

Account details. Your name, phone number or email, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), profile photo and role (owner, buyer, tenant or broker).

Listings and posts. Property details, photos, price, location, descriptions and anything else you publish. Listings and public profile details are visible to other users by design.

Messages. Chats you send to other users on Portal, and the contact requests you make.

Location. The property location you enter, and your device location only if you allow it in your device settings.

Usage and device data. Pages viewed, searches, listings opened, device type, browser, IP address, crash logs and timestamps.

Payment information. If you buy a paid feature, a third-party payment processor handles your card, UPI or bank details. We receive confirmation of payment and a transaction reference. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

Verification details. If we ask you to verify as a broker or owner, we collect only what the check needs, and only for that check.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your listings and profile
  • To connect you with other users and deliver messages
  • To search, filter and rank listings
  • To take payments and prevent fraud
  • To keep Portal safe, investigate abuse and enforce our terms
  • To fix bugs and improve the product
  • To send service messages such as security alerts, account notices and contact requests
  • To send marketing only if you agree, and you can opt out any time
  • To meet legal obligations

3. Who sees your data

Other users. Your public profile and listings are visible to anyone on Portal. Your phone number and email are shared with another user only through the contact flow in the app or when you choose to share them yourself.

Service providers. We use vendors to run Portal: cloud hosting and database services, payment processors, messaging and notification services, analytics and error tracking, and AI service providers that power features such as search assistance and lead qualification. They process data only on our instructions and only to provide their service.

Legal and safety. We disclose data when a law, court order or valid government request requires it, or when needed to protect users, prevent fraud or defend our rights.

Business changes. If Portal is merged, acquired or restructured, your data may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy. We will tell you first.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Messages and privacy between users

Chats on Portal are between you and the other person. We do not read them for advertising. We may access them only to investigate a report of abuse, fraud or illegal activity, to meet a legal request, or to operate an AI feature you turned on. The other person can screenshot or share what you send, so do not share sensitive documents or financial details in chat unless you trust them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except what we must keep for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute reasons. Content you already shared with others, such as a message they received, may remain with them.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and monitoring to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

7. Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Correct or update inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data and your account
  • Withdraw consent for processing that depends on consent
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India

Email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. You can also edit most details and delete your account inside the app.

8. Children

Portal is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

9. Cookies and similar tools

We use cookies and local storage to keep you logged in, remember settings and understand how Portal is used. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of Portal may stop working.

10. Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data on servers in India or in other countries. Wherever it is handled, we require the same level of protection described here and follow the transfer rules in Indian law.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will notify you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top always shows the latest version.
